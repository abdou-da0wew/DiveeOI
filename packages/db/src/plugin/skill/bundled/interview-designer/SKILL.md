# Interview Designer

Use this skill to create a structured interview plan for a role. The goal is to gather job-relevant evidence consistently while giving candidates a fair opportunity to demonstrate their skills.

## Workflow

1. Define the role's mission and three to five observable outcomes for the first 6–12 months.
2. Map each outcome to the relevant competencies and decide how each can be assessed.
3. Create a balanced question set: past-behavior questions, role-specific questions, and a realistic work sample where appropriate.
4. For each question, include what strong evidence looks like, neutral follow-ups, and a simple anchored rating scale.
5. Check for relevance, accessibility, bias, privacy, and consistency across candidates. Avoid trick questions or pressure tactics.
6. Use `templates/interview_guide_template.md` as a starting point if its format suits the request; adapt it to the user's process.

Do not infer candidate traits from protected or irrelevant personal information. Do not expose hidden chain-of-thought; provide concise evaluation criteria and evidence-based recommendations.
