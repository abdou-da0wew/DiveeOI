# Content Strategy

Use this skill when the user asks what content to create, how to organize a content calendar, or how to distribute and evaluate content. Adapt the advice to the user's business, audience, budget, and channels; do not assume a solopreneur or a paid marketing stack.

## Workflow

1. **Set the goal.** Clarify the audience and the primary outcome for the content (awareness, education, leads, sales enablement, retention, or another stated goal).
2. **Understand audience needs.** Use user-provided customer questions, support topics, product documentation, and authorized research. Do not invent interviews, search-volume metrics, competitor findings, or customer evidence.
3. **Define content pillars.** Choose a small number of durable topics that align with the audience's needs and the user's expertise or product.
4. **Select formats and channels.** Recommend a sustainable mix based on effort, reach, shelf life, and the user's capabilities. Explain trade-offs rather than prescribing a universal number of channels.
5. **Plan distribution and reuse.** Identify how each piece will be published, adapted, and linked to related content.
6. **Measure.** Choose a few metrics tied to the goal and establish a baseline before suggesting targets.
7. **Deliver.** Provide a prioritized strategy or calendar. Save a file only when requested.

## Quality rules

- Separate observed audience evidence from hypotheses.
- Prefer a realistic publishing cadence over an ambitious one the user cannot maintain.
- Avoid vanity metrics without an explicit relationship to the goal.
- Respect the user's brand, factual claims, privacy, and consent requirements.
