# Interview Answer Frameworks Cheat Sheet

## STAR (universal for behavioral interviews)

```
S - Situation: the background (30s)
T - Task: your task (20s)
A - Action: your specific actions (90s, the highlight)
R - Result: quantified outcome + reflection (30s)
```

## SCQA (opening statement / self-introduction)

```
S - Situation: your current state / background
C - Complication: the change / problem / trigger
Q - Question: the core question
A - Answer: your answer / action
```

Example: a self-introduction can use SCQA:
- S: "I've been doing ___ at Company X for 5 years"
- C: "The business in my last job has matured, and I want to work on something earlier-stage / more challenging"
- Q: "What kind of role fits my next step best?"
- A: "Your ___ role + my ___ experience are a great match, so I'm here for this interview"

## MECE (structured analysis)

Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive.
When breaking down a question, the categories must not overlap and must not leave gaps. Common dimensions:
- Time: short-term / mid-term / long-term
- Users: new / existing / churned
- Business: first-party / third-party / B2B / B2C
- Geography: tier-1 cities / tier-2 cities / lower-tier markets
- Price / volume / channel

## 5W1H (problem diagnosis)

Who / What / When / Where / Why / How
For any vague question, clarify with 5W1H first.

## CIRCLES (PM case questions)

C - Comprehend (clarify)
I - Identify customer (users)
R - Report needs (needs)
C - Cut by priority (priorities)
L - List solutions (options)
E - Evaluate trade-offs (trade-offs)
S - Summarize (recommendation)

## SOAR (for senior candidates describing strategic experience)

S - Situation
O - Obstacle
A - Action
R - Result

Emphasizes the ability to "break through obstacles".

## 3-Part Self-Introduction (the most reliable)

```
1. "I'm ___, currently doing ___ at ___, for ___ years now"
2. "My most recent / most representative project is ___ — in short, ___, and the result was ___"
3. "I've looked into your ___ business, which aligns closely with my ___ experience / interest in ___, so I'm here for this interview"
```

Length: 1-1.5 minutes.

Prepare both the Chinese and English versions (foreign companies / investment banks / consulting / overseas roles often test English).

## Standard Template for Discussing "Weaknesses"

```
1. A real weakness (not a humble-brag strength)
2. How you became aware of it
3. A consequence it once caused (tell one specific small incident)
4. What you are doing / have done to improve
```

Examples:
- "I used to be too indirect when voicing disagreement. In one design review I disagreed with the PM's direction but didn't say so explicitly; the launch underperformed, and only then did I realize it."
- "Now I deliberately write up 'my objection + supporting data' to the PM after every design discussion — writing it down first, then talking it through — so my communication is clearer and less emotional."

## Standard Template for Discussing "Career Plans"

```
1. Short term (1-2 years): deliver ___ results in this role
2. Mid term (3-5 years): go deep in ___ , growing from IC to senior IC / a small-team lead
3. Long term: become an expert / business owner in the ___ domain
```

Avoid:
- "Becoming your director" (too ambitious, comes across as insincere)
- "Ask me again in 5 years" (sounds like you have no plan)
- "Starting a business / switching careers / moving abroad" (HR will reject you on the spot)
