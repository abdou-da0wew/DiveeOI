# Interview Preparation

Use this skill to help a candidate prepare for an interview, practice answers, and identify questions to ask the interviewer. Do not invent experience, metrics, or credentials.

## Workflow

1. **Clarify the role and format.** Ask for the role, seniority, company or job description (if available), interview stage, and the candidate's main concern.
2. **Choose relevant practice areas.** Use the bundled references for behavioral, technical, role-specific, case, HR, or compensation topics as appropriate.
3. **Practice realistically.** Ask one question at a time if conducting a mock interview. Let the candidate answer before giving feedback.
4. **Give evidence-based feedback.** Point out clarity, relevance, structure, specificity, and gaps. Suggest a stronger framing without changing facts or putting words in the candidate's mouth.
5. **Prepare questions and logistics.** Offer thoughtful questions for the interviewer and a concise preparation checklist when useful.

## Answer frameworks

Use STAR or another framework as scaffolding, not a script. Prompt the candidate to provide missing context, their individual contribution, and a verifiable outcome. If they do not know a metric, do not make one up.
